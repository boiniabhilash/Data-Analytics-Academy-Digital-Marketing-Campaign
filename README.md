# Data Analytics Academy — Digital Marketing Campaign

## Overview

This portfolio project presents an end-to-end digital marketing campaign strategy for **Data Analytics Academy**, a hypothetical online education brand targeting aspiring Data Analytics learners in India.

The campaign is designed to generate prospective-student leads through Meta Ads using a **Free Data Analytics Career Workshop** as the lead-generation offer.

### Campaign Journey

**Meta Ads → Landing Page → Free Workshop → Lead → Nurturing → Course Consideration → Enrollment**

---

## Business Objective

Generate qualified prospective-student leads and create a structured pathway from initial advertising interaction to course consideration.

---

## Target Audience

The campaign focuses on three primary personas:

* **Students (20–23):** Exploring their first career after graduation.
* **Recent Graduates (22–27):** Looking to develop practical Data Analytics skills and portfolio evidence.
* **Career Switchers (25–35):** Exploring a transition toward Data Analytics.

---

## Positioning

### Don't Just Complete a Course. Build Evidence.

The learning framework is:

**LEARN → PRACTICE → BUILD → PREPARE**

* **Learn:** Build foundational skills.
* **Practice:** Work with datasets and business problems.
* **Build:** Create projects and portfolio evidence.
* **Prepare:** Develop resume, portfolio and interview readiness.

---

## Marketing Strategy

The campaign uses a temperature-based funnel:

**Cold Audience**

Students, recent graduates and career switchers are introduced to Data Analytics through educational and career-focused content.

↓

**Warm Audience**

Video viewers, social engagers and website visitors receive consideration-focused messaging.

↓

**Retargeting**

High-intent visitors and form openers receive workshop-focused follow-up messaging.

↓

**Lead Nurturing**

Workshop leads receive a structured 7-day nurture sequence.

↓

**Course Consideration**

Engaged and high-intent leads are introduced to the job-ready program.

## Lead Generation Offer

### Free Data Analytics Career Workshop

The workshop is designed as a lower-friction entry point for prospective learners.

The offer focuses on:

* Skills & tools
* Portfolio projects
* Career roadmap
* Resume preparation
* Interview preparation

---

## Landing Page

The landing page is designed around a single conversion objective:

**Reserve Your Free Seat**

The page includes:

* Hero section
* Problem identification
* Workshop value
* Workshop agenda
* Audience segments
* Learning framework
* FAQ
* Registration form
* Final CTA

---

## Lead Nurturing

A 7-day nurture journey was designed:

**Confirmation → Education → Portfolio Value → Workshop Reminder → Program Introduction → Value → Objection Handling → Conversion**

Lead segments are also considered for students, graduates and career switchers.

---

## Measurement Framework

Key funnel metrics include:

* CTR
* CPC
* CPL
* Landing Page Conversion Rate
* Qualified Lead Rate
* Cost per Qualified Lead
* CAC
* ROAS

Optimization is based on the stage where performance drops rather than relying only on low-cost clicks.

---

## A/B Testing

Five planned experiments were developed:

1. Hook
2. Creative Format
3. Audience
4. Landing Page Headline
5. CTA

Each test defines the variable, primary KPI and learning objective before launch.
