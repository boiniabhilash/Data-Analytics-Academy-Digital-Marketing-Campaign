# Market Research Summary

## Strategic observation
The category frequently communicates career outcomes, technical skills, structured learning and practical development. Data Analytics Academy therefore avoids competing only on course length, number of tools, price or certificate language.

## Proposed differentiation
**Learn → Practice → Build → Prepare**

The campaign repeatedly connects skills to practice, portfolio evidence and career preparation.

## Offer
The free Data Analytics Career Workshop is the acquisition entry point because it asks the prospect for a smaller commitment than directly selling a ₹24,999 program from a cold ad.

## Methodology
This is portfolio research and strategy development. It is not a claim of exhaustive market sizing or a live competitor-performance benchmark.
